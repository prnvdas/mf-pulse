Where this could be

Illustration only, not a forecast. Rows are calendar year-ends; the first counts only the SIPs still to come this year. Each column compounds today's value and the SIP at a fixed annual return with no interruptions, fund switches or SIP changes. 12% is a common long-run planning assumption; 15%, 18% and 22% are optimistic scenarios — sustaining 22% for decades would be exceptional. Real markets don't move in a straight line, and results can differ a lot. Edit the scenarios under projection in config/portfolio.yaml.

How this works. AMFI publishes NAV once daily, after close. Pressing refresh prices every stock in each fund's disclosed portfolio, weights the moves, models the untracked tail with the fund's benchmark index, subtracts the day's TER accrual, and infers where NAV lands tonight.
Read it as a direction, not a settlement figure. Holdings come from monthly AMC disclosures and lag reality. The accuracy panel shows measured error against published NAV — trust that over the headline precision. Nothing here is investment advice.
